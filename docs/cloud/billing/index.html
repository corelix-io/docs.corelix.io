How Billing Works​

  • Billing cycle — Monthly, starting from your sign-up date.
  • Proration — Adding or removing servers mid-cycle is prorated. You pay only for the days used.
  • Plan changes — Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
  • Server billing — Billed from the moment of provisioning until deletion.

Payment Methods​

Payments are processed securely through Stripe:

  • Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • SEPA direct debit (EU bank accounts)

Invoices and Receipts​

  • Invoices are generated automatically each billing cycle.
  • Download invoices from Settings > Billing > Invoice History.
  • Invoices include a breakdown of platform fees and server costs.
  • EU customers receive VAT-compliant invoices with your company details.

Managing Your Plan​

Upgrading​

  1. Go to Settings > Billing.
  2. Click Change Plan.
  3. Select your new plan.
  4. Confirm. The upgrade takes effect immediately with prorated charges.

Downgrading​

  1. Go to Settings > Billing.
  2. Click Change Plan.
  3. Select the lower plan.
  4. The downgrade takes effect at your next billing cycle. Your current features remain active until then.
Before downgrading

Ensure your usage fits within the new plan's limits (number of apps, team members, etc.). Resources exceeding the new limits won't be deleted, but you won't be able to create new ones.

Cancellation​

  1. Go to Settings > Billing.
  2. Click Cancel Subscription.
  3. Your account remains active until the end of the current billing cycle.
  4. After cancellation, servers are preserved for 7 days. You can export data during this period.
  5. After the grace period, managed servers are decommissioned.
No lock-in

Your code and data are always yours. Export your data at any time, or use the open-source community tools to self-host.
